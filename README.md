# Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end retail sales and profitability analysis developed using Microsoft Excel. The project analyzes 10,194 retail transaction records to understand sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, discount patterns, and time-based sales trends. An interactive dashboard was developed using KPI cards, PivotTables, PivotCharts, charts, and slicers to convert raw transaction data into actionable business insights.

## Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large volumes of transaction data, but raw sales records alone do not clearly reveal where revenue is being generated, which products and customers are profitable, where losses occur, or how discounts and seasonal patterns affect performance. The objective of this project was to transform raw retail transaction data into actionable business insights using Excel.

## Project Objectives

- Measure overall sales, profit, quantity, and profit margin.
- Analyze sales and profitability across categories and sub-categories.
- Evaluate customer sales, profitability, margins, and order frequency.
- Identify high-performing and loss-making products.
- Analyze the relationship between discount levels and profitability.
- Identify sales trends, seasonal patterns, and areas requiring business attention.

## Methodology

The project followed an end-to-end Excel analytics workflow including raw data inspection, data quality validation, data cleaning, exploratory analysis, KPI development, PivotTable analysis, customer and product analysis, discount analysis, time-based analysis, dashboard development, business insights, and recommendations.

## Key Findings

- Total sales were **₹23,26,534.35**, with total profit of **₹2,92,296.81** and an overall profit margin of **12.56%**.
- Technology generated the highest category sales and profit, with a **17.45% profit margin**.
- Furniture generated **₹7,54,747.76** in sales but only **₹19,730.00** in profit, resulting in a **2.61% margin**.
- Tables generated **₹2,08,020.18** in sales but recorded a **₹17,753.21 loss**, resulting in a **-8.53% margin**.
- Sales increased from **₹4,94,040.21 in 2023** to **₹7,45,567.53 in 2026**, although sales declined in 2024 before recovering in 2025 and 2026.
- Customer sales volume did not always correspond to profitability. Sean Miller generated **₹25,043.05** in sales but recorded a **₹1,980.74 loss**.
- Several high-sales products generated negative profit, highlighting the importance of evaluating profitability alongside revenue.
- Higher discount levels were associated with lower profitability in this dataset, particularly at extreme discount levels.
- Q4 generated the highest aggregated quarterly sales at approximately **₹8.90 lakh**, while Q1 generated the lowest at approximately **₹3.66 lakh**.

## Business Recommendations

1. Review pricing, discount levels, and cost structure for Tables.
2. Investigate high-sales products generating negative profit.
3. Review discount policies, especially at higher discount levels.
4. Identify and support products and sub-categories with strong profit margins.
5. Evaluate customers using profitability and margins, not sales volume alone.
6. Align inventory and sales planning with stronger second-half sales patterns.
7. Investigate Furniture profitability while maintaining growth in Technology and Office Supplies.
8. Evaluate customer relationships using both order frequency and profitability.
9. Monitor large month-to-month sales changes to improve demand planning.
10. Strengthen category-level planning for the high-performing Q4 period.

## Project Outcome

This project transformed 10,194 raw retail transaction records into a structured Excel analytics solution. The analysis identified key sales and profitability patterns across categories, customers, products, discounts, and time periods. An interactive dashboard was developed with KPI cards, 16 analytical charts, and slicers to support business performance monitoring. The project also produced documented insights and actionable recommendations focused on improving profitability, managing discounts, investigating loss-making products, and supporting business planning.
